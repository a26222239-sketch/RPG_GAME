# 原始繪圖提示詞

角色與殿景使用 ChatGPT 內建圖像生成功能繪製。初期造型與全盛修稿均取第一張燕回全身稿作人物參考。共用的中文版提示詞收於 [guide.md](guide.md)。面板、按鈕與氣血條依像素規格獨立繪製。

## 燕回全盛基礎稿

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create one master character cutout for an original side-view Korean murim manhwa RPG. Tall 2:3 portrait canvas, desired at least 1024 × 1536. A SINGLE adult male sword master, Yan Hui at the height of his powers, in his late twenties, grounded, quietly protective, one faint healed nick by the eyebrow, composed closed mouth, narrow observant eyes. Show his WHOLE standing body, hair, boots, weapon tip and every loose cloth tail safely inside the canvas, clear empty margins, boots level. Grounded athletic seven-and-a-half-head proportions. Camera exactly level, flat orthographic; torso and feet turned toward the RIGHT of the picture; face right in near profile, one eye clearly readable, elegant nose silhouette. NO front-facing portrait. Center hips on canvas. He stands still, chin level, weight on rear leg, shoulders relaxed. Near hand naturally grips the hilt of his one unsheathed, slender Chinese straight double-edged jian sword, held low with the blade pointing diagonally down and forward to picture right. Distinctively straight blade, single hilt and bronze guard. Far hand relaxed; avoid unnecessary scabbard clutter. A black high tied ponytail with just a few freed strands; cracked pale-celadon open ring on a short woven cord on the near hip, as a recognizable keepsake. Period crossover robes: ink-blue long fitted wrap coat with a strong clear waist, one asymmetric pale bone-coloured short shoulder mantle thrown back to the left, desaturated pine-teal lined trailing split hems, muted antique brass only on fittings. Worn hems from long travel. A restrained sliver of pale celadon inner light caught in the edge of the sleeve; no floating aura graphic. Sophisticated hand-drawn, original Korean manhwa art direction: calligraphic tapered ink outer line, finer cloth seams, strong shaped shadows in two flat painterly planes, selective dry texture in fabric only, a small crisp upper-left rim light. Dark jade and indigo base with pale textile for value separation, clear at tiny tactical RPG scale, expressive adult face, beautiful economical brush line. Restrained, disciplined, distinctive costume, not a busy gacha costume. Original design. A clean isolated asset with REAL TRANSPARENT background, no floor or contact shadow. No environment, text, labels, signatures, palette swatches, layout frame, checkerboard, particles, floating lettering or decorative cards.
```

```text

Return a single full-length right-facing cutout on TRUE TRANSPARENT background in a 1024 x 1536 tall 2:3 canvas, no ground shadow. Show head, tip and both boot soles fully. Keep the near-hip small circular pale celadon jade ring on an ordinary brown cord; it is the same physical pendant, with its hole visible.
